Materials Science & Embedded Systems

Perovskite photovoltaics, engineered as a complete system

Perovskite solar materials offer unprecedented efficiency ceilings and thin-film versatility. Realizing that potential in high-consequence defence and aerospace environments requires treating the crystal cell, the charge electronics, and the control firmware as one indivisible engineering problem.

Physics & Architecture

Materials science meets embedded engineering

Unlike rigid crystalline silicon, perovskite thin films exhibit rapid charge-carrier dynamics, tunable bandgaps, and distinct current-voltage (I-V) hysteresis. Standard solar inverters and generic charge controllers misinterpret these dynamics, resulting in rapid power oscillation and thermal degradation.

Avimukt's engineering methodology addresses four integrated domains:

  • Perovskite crystal composition & unencapsulated stability formulation
  • Vacuum & stratospheric UV environmental resilience engineering
  • Dedicated charge-control electronics tuned to perovskite I-V curves
  • Real-time telemetry, fault clamping, and operational data logging
