Technology

Perovskite photovoltaics, engineered as a complete system

Our technology work spans the perovskite solar cell material itself and the embedded electronics that turn it into a deployable power source.

Technology Overview

Materials science meets embedded engineering

Perovskite solar cells offer a path to high-efficiency, lower-cost photovoltaics compared to conventional silicon approaches. Realising that potential in a working system means treating the cell, the charge electronics, and the control firmware as one integrated design problem — not three separate ones.

Our technology overview spans:

  • Perovskite solar cell composition and device architecture
  • Photovoltaic material characterisation and process development
  • Charge-control and power electronics matched to cell behaviour
  • Embedded firmware for monitoring, protection and communication
