Hardware Pillar 01 • Flagship Architecture

Perovskite Solar Cell Charge Controller

A dedicated charge-control architecture built specifically around the input characteristics of perovskite solar cells — covering everything from raw cell input to protected, monitored, and logged output to high-altitude payloads or test rigs.

Hardware Integrity

Built for perovskite, not adapted from silicon

Generic solar charge controllers are designed around the electrical behavior and slower thermal dynamics of conventional silicon cells. Avimukt's controller is engineered specifically around perovskite solar-cell input characteristics — ensuring that the charging stage, monitoring thresholds, and protection logic are calibrated to the crystal photovoltaic technology it serves.

Because operating parameters are programmable and the platform supports digital communication (UART/I2C/CAN bus), the same core controller adapts across cleanroom research bench setups, prototype high-altitude balloons, and fielded defence systems.

Control Architecture

The Eight Core Capabilities

A closed-loop power control architecture linking raw photovoltaic input to regulated destination storage with four surrounding telemetry and safety layers.

CHARGE CONTROLLER FUNCTIONAL SIGNAL FLOW CLOSED LOOP PROTOCOL
CAPABILITY 1

Solar-Cell Input Interface

Accepts the raw electrical output of the perovskite solar cell as the controller's front-end input stage.

CAPABILITY 2

Controlled Charging Stage

Manages the charging profile applied to the connected battery or load, tuned to the cell's I-V curve.

CAPABILITY 3

Voltage & Current Monitoring

Continuously tracks voltage and current through the charging path to inform high-speed control decisions.

CAPABILITY 4

Programmable Parameters

Operating thresholds and algorithms can be configured for the specific deployment or test scenario.

CAPABILITY 5

Protection & Fault Management

Guards the charging path and connected load against overvoltage, reverse current, and thermal surges.

CAPABILITY 6

Data Acquisition & Logging

Captures real-time operational data over time for performance diagnostics, analysis, and telemetry reporting.

CAPABILITY 7

Digital Comms / UI Interface

Provides a standardized digital interface (UART/I2C/CAN) to observe status and exchange data with external flight computers.

CAPABILITY 8

Custom Platform Integration

Adapts the controller's output stage to specific battery chemistries (Li-ion, LiFePO4) or test bench rigs.
