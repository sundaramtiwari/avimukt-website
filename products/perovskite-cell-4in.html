Perovskite Solar Cells

4-Inch Perovskite Solar Cell — Stratospheric Grade

An environmentally stable perovskite solar cell that operates without encapsulation, specially designed for the stratospheric environment — where extreme UV exposure, low pressure and wide temperature swings would degrade an unprotected cell of conventional design.

Product Photos

The 4-inch cell

Lab photograph of the fabricated cell. Encapsulation-free by design — see “How It Works” below.

Why No Encapsulation

Environmental stability built into the cell, not added on top

Perovskite solar cells are normally sealed behind a glass or polymer encapsulation layer to protect the active material from moisture, oxygen and thermal stress — a layer that adds weight, thickness and a failure point of its own.

This 4-inch cell is formulated and processed to be environmentally stable on its own, without that added encapsulation. That makes it materially lighter and thinner than an encapsulated cell of the same size, which matters directly for the stratospheric platforms it is designed for — high-altitude balloons and similar craft, where every gram of payload counts and the cell must survive intense UV, near-vacuum pressure and extreme temperature swings unprotected.

How It Works

From light to usable charge

The perovskite active layer absorbs incoming sunlight and generates a voltage across the cell's electrodes, the same basic principle as any photovoltaic cell. What is different here is the cell chemistry and construction: it is engineered to keep working, unsealed, in conditions — intense UV, near-vacuum, wide temperature swings — that would normally require a protective encapsulation layer.

Pair it with Avimukt's Perovskite Solar Cell Charge Controller, tuned to this cell's input characteristics, to turn that raw output into regulated, protected charge for a battery or downstream platform.

Key Characteristics

Designed for the stratosphere

Environmentally stable, unencapsulated

Built for the stratospheric environment
