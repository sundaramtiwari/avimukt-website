About Avimukt Engineers

Advancing perovskite solar technology, one working system at a time

We develop and deliver advanced perovskite solar cells designed to support the future of high-efficiency, sustainable, and affordable photovoltaic energy.

Our Company

Who we are

Avimukt Engineers Pvt Ltd is focused on the research, development, and commercialization of next-generation solar technologies. Our core work centers on perovskite solar cells and related photovoltaic materials.

We bridge advanced materials science with practical energy solutions — and, alongside the cell technology itself, we design the embedded controllers and power electronics needed to actually put that technology to work in the field.

Our team brings expertise across materials engineering, device fabrication, process development, and product innovation.

Our Mission

To advance solar technology through innovative perovskite-based solutions that combine efficiency, affordability, and sustainability.

Our Values

Innovation

Continuously improving materials, device architecture and manufacturing methods.

Quality

Reliable product development with strong performance standards.

Sustainability

Supporting cleaner energy adoption across the programmes we serve.
