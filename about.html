Who We Are

Bridging advanced materials with deployable electronics

Avimukt Engineers was founded with a singular conviction: high-efficiency perovskite photovoltaics will only transform field operations if they are engineered alongside the embedded controllers and power electronics that put them to work.

Our core team brings cross-disciplinary capability spanning materials engineering, device fabrication, thin-film process development, embedded firmware, and ruggedized power stage design.
