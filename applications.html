Engineering solutions for defence, aerospace & research programmes

Avimukt bridges high-efficiency perovskite thin-film photovoltaics and custom power electronics across high-altitude flights, tactical field operations, and advanced laboratory characterization testbeds.

AEROSPACE ENVELOPE

High-Altitude & Stratospheric Craft

At stratospheric altitudes (15 km to 35 km), conventional glass-encapsulated solar panels impose severe weight penalties. Avimukt's unencapsulated 4-inch cell formulation withstands intense raw UV exposure, near-vacuum pressure, and extreme thermal cycling without dead weight, providing maximum power-to-mass ratio for zero-pressure high-altitude balloons and pseudo-satellites.

DEFENCE & TACTICAL

Tactical Soldier Gear & Conformal Structures

Warfighters and expeditionary units operate in remote areas where carrying spare battery weight creates fatigue. Avimukt's 2-inch conformal cells mount directly to combat uniforms, tent canopies, or vehicle armor, wiring into strings that feed into our dedicated charge controller to recharge radios, GPS trackers, and thermal sensors in the field.

SCIENTIFIC RIGS

Research Laboratories & Characterization Benches

Cleanrooms and photovoltaic test laboratories require precise control over solar-cell operating points. Our charge controller features programmable thresholds, high-speed telemetry, and data logging to observe and record real-time perovskite cell performance under simulated solar spectra and environmental chambers.

AUTONOMOUS IOT

Remote & Autonomous Off-Grid Power

Off-grid infrastructure, seismic monitoring stations, and autonomous ocean buoys require ultra-reliable trickle charging and fault protection. Avimukt's integrated power chain ensures consistent energy capture and battery longevity without field maintenance.
