Engineering Truth

Why perovskite demands purpose-built hardware

Adapting legacy silicon electronics to perovskite photovoltaics results in mismatched impedance, sluggish thermal tracking, and premature cell stress. Here is why Avimukt designs the full stack.

Avimukt Innovation

Unencapsulated Environmental Stability

Conventional cells rely on heavy glass/polymer seals to prevent ambient moisture and oxygen breakdown. In the stratosphere, that sealing layer adds parasitic dead weight and creates a thermal expansion failure point under extreme temperature swings (-60°C to +40°C).

Avimukt's 4-inch cell chemistry is formulated to operate unsealed, delivering maximum power-to-weight ratio where every gram of payload affects balloon altitude and flight duration.

Avimukt Innovation

Tuned Charge Control vs Silicon Adaptations

Standard MPPT controllers are hardcoded around crystalline silicon I-V response curves. When paired with perovskite cells, they oscillate during transient cloud shading and fail to maintain optimal operating voltage.

Avimukt's charge controller is designed from the PCB traces up around perovskite electrical characteristics, featuring programmable operating thresholds and high-speed fault clamping.
