2-Inch Perovskite Solar Cell — High-Altitude / Conformal

An environmentally stable perovskite solar cell designed specifically for conformal integration onto fabric, canopy canvas, or curved metal surfaces. Interconnected cell strings charge tactical field gear and remote sensors when paired with Avimukt's dedicated charge controller.

Single 2-Inch Cell Format

Fabricated active sample showing gold-plated edge contact tabs for low-resistance series/parallel string interconnections.

Fabricated Production Batch

Multiple lab-grown cells assembled and ready for conformal stringing onto tactical fabrics or platform structures.

Substrate Versatility

One cell format, any operational surface

The 2-inch cell is built to mount onto whatever geometry the platform requires — woven into or bonded onto flexible textiles for wearable soldier uniforms and tent canopies, or bonded to curved titanium and carbon-fiber hulls.

Individual cells are compact by design. To generate mission-grade power, multiple cells are wired in series and/or parallel into an interconnected array — the same way conventional solar panels combine many small cells — sized precisely to the target device load.

SYSTEM INTEGRATION

From Surface to Charged Device

Each 2-inch cell converts incident light into electrical potential across its two contact tabs. The interconnected array output feeds directly into Avimukt's Perovskite Charge Controller, which regulates, protects, and logs the power to charge field radios, GPS beacons, or battery packs safely.
