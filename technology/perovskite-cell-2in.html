Perovskite Solar Cells

2-Inch Perovskite Solar Cell — High-Altitude & Conformal

An environmentally stable perovskite solar cell designed specifically for high-altitude defence or civilian applications. The cell can be integrated onto any surface — fabric or metal — and interconnected cells can be used to charge handheld devices when paired with Avimukt's charge controller, built specifically for these perovskite cells.

Cell Photos

The 2-inch cell

Lab-fabricated samples, shown individually and as a production batch.

Conformal by Design

One cell, any surface

The 2-inch cell is built to be mounted on whatever surface the platform requires — woven into or bonded onto fabric for wearable and canopy-integrated use, or fixed to a rigid metal surface for a structural mount. That flexibility is what makes it suited to both high-altitude defence platforms and civilian applications that need power generated directly on the surface of the equipment itself.

Individual 2-inch cells are small by design. To generate meaningful power, multiple cells are interconnected into a string or array — the same way conventional solar panels combine many small cells — sized to the load, whether that is a sensor package or a handheld device.

How It Works

From surface to charged device

Each 2-inch cell converts incident light into a small voltage and current at its two contact tabs. Cells are wired in series and/or parallel — an interconnected array — to reach the voltage and current a target load needs.

That array feeds into Avimukt's Perovskite Solar Cell Charge Controller, purpose-built for this cell's electrical characteristics, which regulates and protects the output so it can safely charge a battery or, directly, a handheld device.
