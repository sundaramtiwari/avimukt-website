4-Inch Perovskite Solar Cell — Stratospheric Grade

An environmentally stable perovskite solar cell that operates without encapsulation, specially formulated for the stratospheric environment — where intense UV radiation, near-vacuum pressure, and wide temperature swings would degrade unprotected conventional cells.

Why No Encapsulation

Environmental stability built into the active layer, not sealed on top

Perovskite solar cells are normally sealed behind a glass or polymer encapsulation barrier to protect the active crystal material from ambient moisture, oxygen, and thermal stress — a layer that adds parasitic weight, increases thickness, and creates a critical failure point of its own during thermal cycling.

Avimukt's 4-inch cell is formulated and processed to achieve inherent environmental stability without that added encapsulation. This makes it substantially lighter and thinner than an encapsulated cell of equivalent active area.

This directly benefits high-altitude balloon platforms and stratospheric pseudo-satellites, where every single gram of payload budget dictates flight altitude, station-keeping duration, and scientific capability.

Design Envelope

Engineered for the Stratospheric Regime

Unencapsulated Stability

Retains photo-conversion stability without the heavy glass/polymer sealing layers conventional perovskite cells require.

Extreme Environmental Resistance

Engineered to withstand intense raw UV exposure, near-vacuum low pressure, and rapid thermal swings (-60°C to +40°C).

4-Inch Active Area

A single-cell monolithic format optimized for direct mounting onto stratospheric balloon payload trusses and test pods.
